Our Story

A Taste to Remember.

At Neeracakesandmore, we believe good food brings people together and makes every celebration more memorable. What started as a passion for creating delicious Nigerian small chops has grown into a business offering finger foods, small chops, cakes, and beautifully presented treats for all kinds of events.

From intimate celebrations to weddings, birthdays, baby showers, and special occasions, we put care, flavor, and beautiful presentation into everything we create. Our goal is simple: to give you and your guests A Taste to Remember.
