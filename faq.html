FAQ

Quick answers before you order.

How do I order?

Add items to your cart and send your request. It goes to Neera on WhatsApp and she confirms with you directly.

Do I pay when I send a request?

No. Nothing is charged until Neera approves your request and you agree the details with her.

How early should I order?

As early as you can. Include your date in the request and Neera will confirm whether she is available.

Do you make custom cakes?

Yes. Add a cake to your request and tell us the size, flavour, design and date. Cakes are priced on request.

Do you cater events?

Yes. We cater weddings, birthdays, baby showers and other special occasions. Add Event Catering to your request for a quote.

Is it pickup or delivery?

Choose your preference when you send your request. Neera will confirm what is possible for your date and location.

What do your small chops include?

Puff-puff, samosas, spring rolls and more. See the menu for what is currently available.

Where are you based?

Atlanta, Georgia.
