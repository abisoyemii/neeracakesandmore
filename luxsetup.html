About the Lux Set-Up

Beyond trays and platters, NeeraCakesAndMore builds a full grazing and display set-up at your venue: cakes, small chops, finger foods and sweets arranged as one styled table, ready before your guests arrive.

At Neeracakesandmore, we believe good food brings people together and makes every celebration more memorable. The Lux Set-Up brings that same care to the table itself, so the display is as beautifully presented as the food on it. Tell us your guest count, date, theme and venue, and we will put together a set-up designed for your celebration.
